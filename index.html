June Speaking Challenge

10 Days to Boost Your English Fluency (Level A2-B2)

How to Participate

Complete these 10 challenges at your own pace anytime during the month of June. Aim to speak for 1 to 2 minutes per challenge.

  1. Read the prompt for the day.
  2. Take 1 minute to think about your answer and make brief notes (don't write a full script!).
  3. Click the button below to open Vocaroo, record your voice, and save it.
  4. Copy the Vocaroo link and send it to me via Preply chat for feedback.

Day 1

My Daily Routine

Describe your typical morning routine. What is the first thing you do? How does it help you prepare for the day?

Vocabulary tip: Use sequence words like 'first', 'then', 'after that', and 'finally'.
Day 2

A Memorable Trip

Talk about a place you visited in the past that you really enjoyed. Where did you go, who were you with, and what did you do?

Grammar tip: Focus on using the past simple tense correctly.
Day 3

My Favorite Hobby

What do you like to do in your free time? How long have you been doing it, and why does it make you happy?

Grammar tip: Try to use the present perfect (e.g., "I have played guitar for two years").
Day 4

Future Goals

Where do you see yourself in three years? Think about your career, your travel plans, or your English learning journey.

Grammar tip: Practice using 'will', 'be going to', and 'hope to'.
Day 5

Home Sweet Home

Describe your favorite room in your house or apartment. What does it look like, and why do you like spending time there?

Vocabulary tip: Use prepositions of place (next to, across from, behind) and descriptive adjectives.
Day 6

The Best Advice

What is the best piece of advice you have ever received? Who gave it to you, and how did it change your perspective?

Grammar tip: Practice reporting what someone said using past tenses or modal verbs (should, ought to).
Day 7

A Great Movie or Book

Summarize a movie you recently watched or a book you read. What was the main plot, and would you recommend it to others?

Vocabulary tip: Try using descriptive adjectives like 'gripping', 'hilarious', or 'thought-provoking'.
Day 8

Food and Culture

If I visited your country, what traditional dish must I try? Describe what it tastes like and how it is made.

Grammar tip: Use the passive voice if you can (e.g., "It is served with...", "The ingredients are mixed...").
Day 9

Healthy Habits

What is one thing you do to take care of your physical or mental health? How do you stay motivated to do it?

Vocabulary tip: Use frequency adverbs like 'usually', 'rarely', or 'every once in a while'.
Day 10

An Ideal Weekend

If you had no work, no chores, and an unlimited budget, how would you spend your perfect Saturday and Sunday?

Grammar tip: Use the second conditional (e.g., "If I had more time, I would...").

Good luck! I look forward to listening to your recordings on Preply.
